**p4n4-dashboard 1.2.0** is a plain-language redesign in English and Spanish, with a Feed tab, the project's own Home page, and an assistant that goes through p4n4-api.

```bash
docker pull ghcr.io/raisga/p4n4-dashboard:1.2.0    # amd64 and arm64
```

> **Trusted networks only,** like the rest of p4n4 0.3.x. Keep port 8088 on your LAN. If it must be reachable from elsewhere, serve it over HTTPS (`docker-compose.tls.yml`) with basic auth on.

1.1.1 was never released; its configurable restart policy ships in 1.2.0.

## Highlights

- **Plain-language redesign:** every view starts on **Home**: one "everything is working / something needs attention" card, the edge device's readings, then the project's `HOME.md` (or `HOME.es.md`). Its `tab:` links open a tab and `ask:` links ask the Assistant. Normies get a read-only viewer and a short first-run tour.
- **Languages and accessibility:** English and Spanish, following the device unless **Settings → Language & region** overrides it, with temperature unit and 12/24-hour time. **Accessibility** adds text size, high contrast and reduced motion. Settings are grouped into searchable categories.
- **Feed tab** (opt-in, `feed` in a brand's `tabs`): the project's live events and alerts, through p4n4-api. Operators and admins act on alerts.
- **Assistant:** chats go through p4n4-api, which keeps the Letta password and holds normies to the model or agent an operator chose. Conversations are kept per user and deployment, and the chat shows each tool the agent runs while it answers.
- **Views kept by the deployment:** admins drag tabs into order and choose each view's tabs in **Settings → Views**. p4n4-api stores them, so they apply on every device.
- **Video:** the phone or tablet's own `back` or `front` camera, video files (`.mp4`, `.webm`, `.mov`, `.m3u8`) besides MJPEG and snapshots, and demo cameras for admins. A brand can bring its own demo clips, served from `DASHBOARD_DEMO_DIR`.
- **`RESTART_POLICY`** in `.env` sets the container's restart policy (default `unless-stopped`; `no` keeps it down until the next `docker compose up -d`).

## Compatibility

- Needs **p4n4-api 0.2.0** for the assistant, views, Feed and Home page.
- Pinned by projects that **p4n4 (CLI) 0.3.0** scaffolds (`DASHBOARD_VERSION=1.2.0`).

## Upgrading from 1.1.0

- Upgrade p4n4-api to 0.2.0 first.
- nginx no longer proxies Ollama or Letta. Remove `OLLAMA_UPSTREAM` and `LETTA_UPSTREAM` from `.env`, and point p4n4-api at them instead (`P4N4_API_OLLAMA_URL`, `P4N4_API_LETTA_URL`).
- The Letta password a device kept in secure storage is removed on first launch; the API keeps it now.
- Tab choices an admin made on a device are handed to p4n4-api on their next sign-in there.
